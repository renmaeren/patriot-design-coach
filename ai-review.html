04 · Use Gemini's feedback to revise your spread

Do not stop when Gemini answers. The goal is to make corrections in Memento, not just receive a review.

  1. Read the entire Gemini response. It should give you THREE waves: 1. Layout, 2. Design, 3. Text/Grammar. If a wave is missing, ask Gemini: “Please finish the missing review wave and list every issue you can actually see.”
  2. Start with Wave 1 — Layout. Find the section labeled MUST DO. Open your spread in Memento and fix those problems first. Then work through NEED TO DO NEXT. Only consider OPTIONAL POLISH after the required work.
  3. Continue with Wave 2 — Design in the same order: MUST DO, NEED TO DO NEXT, OPTIONAL POLISH. Make the changes in Memento—not just in the Gemini conversation.
  4. Finish with Wave 3 — Text/Grammar. Correct readable errors. Independently verify names, quotes, dates, statistics, captions, rosters, and any text Gemini could not clearly read.
  5. Mark corrections in Gemini's checklist. Copy Gemini's response into a Google Doc or your assignment submission, then change each unchecked box [ ] to [x] only after you have made that correction in Memento. Do not mark unchecked items complete merely to finish.
  6. Recheck the finished spread. Export the revised page(s), attach them in the SAME Gemini conversation with the official template, and ask: “Recheck my revised spread against your previous findings. Which MUST DO and NEED TO DO NEXT issues remain? List any new problems you can see.”
  7. Submit for human review. Follow your editor's required review and approval process. Gemini does not approve pages, verify reporting, or replace an adult reviewer.
Priority key: MUST DO = incorrect template use, missing required elements, illegible text, or other problems blocking approval. NEED TO DO NEXT = important quality improvements. OPTIONAL POLISH = small, nonessential Field Guide enhancements. An item can appear in any of the three review waves.

Keep Gemini's response as your revision checklist; you do not have to retype every finding into the manual Spread Inspection Studio.

05 · Recheck My Revised Spread

Already received Gemini feedback and made corrections in Memento? Come back here to start a second review. You do not need to redo the whole guided setup.

  1. Export or screenshot your revised spread from Memento. Keep your original Gemini conversation open or find it in your Gemini history.
  2. Click Copy Recheck Instructions below.
  3. Click Return to Gemini and open the same conversation that contains the first review. A new conversation will not automatically remember the previous feedback.
  4. Paste the recheck instructions, and attach your revised spread and the same official template. If you use a new conversation, also attach or paste your first feedback report.
  5. Review which findings Gemini marks RESOLVED, STILL NEEDS WORK, or CANNOT VERIFY. Correct remaining MUST DO items in Memento, then repeat if necessary.
  6. Submit the revised spread through your editor's normal review process. Only a human reviewer can approve it.
What gets checked? Gemini compares the revised spread against the assigned template and the previous review. It should revisit every original correction and identify newly visible problems, not just give three more suggestions.
